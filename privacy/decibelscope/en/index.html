Decibelscope Privacy Policy

Effective 17 September 2026 · Publisher: Owlmakers · 한국어

Owlmakers ("the developer") operates Decibelscope ("the app"), which uses the microphone to measure sound level (dB) and shows you the error measured on this specific device. It is an on-device app with no sign-in and no cloud.

Summary
· Microphone input is used only to compute sound level — nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
· There is no account or sign-in, so the developer holds no personal data about you. Only anonymous usage statistics are sent to a developer server.
· Ads (Google AdMob) may use device identifiers.
· Long-session logging, CSV export, C-weighted switching and ad removal unlock with a one-time purchase; real-time measurement and the accuracy check are always free.

1. Microphone use — nothing is recorded

The app uses the microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) to measure sound level (dB).

  • Microphone input is used only to compute sound level and is discarded immediately after.
  • No recording is created, stored on the device, or sent anywhere.
  • All analysis happens entirely on your device.
  • The app stops using the microphone when you leave the measurement screen or the app goes to the background.

2. Data stored on your device

The following stays on your device only and is never transmitted.

ItemContents
Measurement recordsTimestamp, dB value, and confidence-range status of each measurement
Calibration valueThis device's microphone offset (the result of accuracy calibration)
App settingsLanguage, theme, unlock state

Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app shows Google AdMob ads to remain free.

  • AdMob may collect an advertising ID (AAID) and device information to serve ads. This is governed by Google's policies; the developer does not separately collect or retain it.
  • Google advertising data: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Ad personalisation can be changed in device Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Users in the EEA and the UK are shown a consent (UMP) dialog on first launch.
  • Ads are removed once you unlock the app with a one-time purchase.

4. Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

This app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics so the app can be improved.

  • What is collected — app usage events such as which screens you open and which features you use, together with an app instance ID that is randomly assigned per installation.
  • What is not collected — no personally identifying information (name, email, phone number), and measurement or calibration values themselves are never sent. The app instance ID is not linked to a person and is discarded when you uninstall the app.
  • Why — to see which features are actually used and prioritise improvements accordingly.
  • Who processes it — Google performs the collection and processing; the developer only sees aggregated statistics in which individuals are not identifiable. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

5. Our own usage statistics (Owlmakers)

To check that the app is working in real use, the developer sends anonymous usage statistics (including country and region) to its own server (api.owlmakers.com, over HTTPS).

  • What is sent: app name, version and display language; screen views and actions such as starting or finishing a measurement; an "in use" signal; and a random value created fresh on every launch (never stored, so it identifies neither you nor your device).
  • The server records only the country, region and city name of the connection. IP addresses are not stored, and measurement values, calibration values, or precise location are never received.
  • Data is kept for one year and is not shared with third parties.

6. Payments — one-time unlock

Long-session logging, graphs, CSV export, switching to C-weighted (dBC) measurement, and ad removal can be unlocked through Google Play in-app billing.

  • Payment details (card numbers and the like) are handled by Google; the developer never receives or sees them.
  • The developer only stores the fact that a purchase exists on the device and uses it to unlock features.
  • This is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it is restored through your Google Play account after a device change or reinstall.
  • Real-time measurement and the accuracy check remain free regardless of this purchase.

7. Children under 14

The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

8. Your rights

The app has no accounts and no sign-in, so the developer holds no personal data about you. You can clear on-device data at any time by deleting records in the app or uninstalling it.

9. Safety notice

This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Use a certified sound level meter where legal accuracy is required. Because of microphone limits, error grows at very high levels (roughly above 100 dB).
