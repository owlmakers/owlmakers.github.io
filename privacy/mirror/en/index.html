Mirror Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · Published by Owlmakers · 한국어

Owlmakers ("the developer") operates Mirror ("the app"). The app is a mirror that uses the front camera to show your face, with screen lighting and photo comparison.

The developer collects no personally identifying data. There is no account, no sign-up and no login, and no user data is stored on a server. The camera preview and your photos are processed on your device only. The only thing sent to a developer server is the anonymous usage statistics in section 2b.

1. Handled on your device only

DataPurposeStored where

About screen brightness and keep-awake — the lighting feature on the mirror screen changes only the app window's brightness and returns to normal when you leave. Keeping the screen on works only while you are on the mirror screen. The app does not change any system settings.

2. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

To show ads, Google may collect device identifiers such as the Advertising ID along with basic device and app information. Google is the party collecting and using that data; the developer does not receive it. Where required (for example in Europe), consent is collected through Google's consent tool (UMP). See the Google advertising policy. You can reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the app is used, the names of actions such as screen views and button taps are sent to Google's Firebase Analytics. Photos, and file paths are not sent. Google collects and processes that data; see the Google Privacy Policy.

2b. Our own usage statistics (Owlmakers)

To check that the app is working in real use, the developer sends anonymous usage statistics (including country and region) to its own server (api.owlmakers.com, over HTTPS).

  • What is sent: app name, version and display language; names of actions such as screen views, button taps and task results (success/failure); whether an ad was shown or clicked; an "in use" signal (every 15 seconds); and a random value created fresh on every launch (never stored, so it identifies neither you nor your device).
  • The server records only the country, region and city name of the connection. IP addresses are not stored, and no precise location is received. Your photos and file paths are not sent here.
  • Statistics that fail to send are kept on your device for up to 7 days and sent again. Data on the server is kept for one year and is not shared with third parties.

4. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

5. Retention and deletion

Because the developer holds no personally identifying data, there is no deletion request procedure. Uninstalling the app removes the photos and settings kept in app-private storage. Copies saved to the device album and the Downloads folder remain after uninstalling, so delete them from your gallery or file app. (The only thing kept on the developer's server is the anonymous usage statistics in section 2b, deleted automatically after one year.)
