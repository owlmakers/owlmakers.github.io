LED Board Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026 · Publisher: Owlmakers · 한국어

Owlmakers ("the developer") operates LED Board (LED 전광판) ("the app"). The app shows the message you type full screen like an LED sign. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and it keeps your messages and settings only on your device.

Summary
· The messages you type and the settings you save stay on your device. They are never sent to the developer's servers or to any third party.
· There are no accounts or sign-ups, and the app does not ask for location, camera, microphone, or contacts permissions.
· The app does use ads (Google AdMob) and anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase and the developer's own server). Your sign messages are not part of either.
· No ads are shown on the full-screen play screen.

1. Your messages and settings — they never leave your device

  • The text you enter, display settings such as colors, speed, and effects, and your saved presets and favorites are never sent to the developer's servers or to any third party.
  • Showing, editing, and saving signs all happen only on your device.
  • Keeping the screen on and maximum brightness during full-screen play only change screen behavior and collect no information.

2. Data stored on your device

The following stays on your device only and is never sent to the developer.

ItemContents
Current sign settingsMessage text, display style, text and background colors, size, speed, direction, effect settings
Saved presetsMessages and settings you saved, and which ones are favorites
Play options & app settingsWhether to use maximum brightness while playing, theme (System / Light / Dark), display language, whether the first-run guide was shown
Automatic backup fileA file named led_board_backup.json containing the information above. It is written to your device's Downloads folder whenever the information changes, so that after reinstalling you can pick it yourself in Settings to restore. It stays on the device, is never sent to the developer, and you can delete it with a file manager.
Temporary share imageWhen you share, a sign image (led_board_share.png) is created in the app's temporary storage.

3. Sharing and the clipboard

  • When you share a sign, the app creates a sign image (PNG) and an OWLLED: setup code and hands them to the app you choose in your device's share sheet (for example, a messenger). The developer does not receive them, and there is no sharing server.
  • The same content is also copied to your device clipboard when you share.
  • When the app returns to the screen, or when you tap Import, it checks on the device whether the clipboard text contains an OWLLED: code. It shows a preview only if one is found. Nothing else on the clipboard is stored or sent.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app displays Google AdMob ads to remain free.

  • Ads may appear in the banner at the top of the main screens (Home, Edit, Presets, Settings), in the exit confirmation dialog, and similar places. No ads are shown on the full-screen play screen.
  • AdMob may collect an advertising ID (AAID), IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic information (such as app launch time) to serve ads. This is governed by Google's policies; the developer does not separately collect or retain it.
  • The developer never shares your messages or saved settings for advertising purposes.
  • Google advertising data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Ad personalisation can be changed in device Settings → Google → Ads.
  • "Ad Privacy Settings" in the app's Settings lets you change your ad consent again (shown only where it applies).
  • Users in the EEA and the UK are shown a consent (UMP) dialog on first launch. The ads SDK is not started until that step is done.

5. Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

This app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics so the app can be improved.

  • What is collected — which screens were opened (the Home, Edit, Presets, and Settings tabs and the full-screen play screen), a randomly assigned app instance ID per installation, and what Google Analytics collects automatically (screen views and sessions, an approximate region derived from the IP address, the advertising ID, and similar).
  • What is not collected — no personally identifying information (name, email, phone number) and none of the message text you enter.
  • Who processes it — Google performs the collection and processing; the developer only views aggregated statistics in which individuals are not identifiable. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. The developer's own usage statistics (Owlmakers)

To check that the app is working, the developer's own server receives anonymous usage statistics (including country and region) (api.owlmakers.com, over HTTPS). This is sent only from official release builds.

  • What is sent: app name, version, display language, the kind of screen movement (tab name, opening the full-screen play screen, and so on), a signal that the app is in use, the fact that an ad was tapped (ad type), and a random value generated fresh every time the app starts (it is not stored, so it does not identify you or your device).
  • What is not sent: your message text, presets, settings, clipboard content, name, email, or phone number.
  • From the connection, the server records only the names of the country, region, and city. It does not store IP addresses, and it does not receive precise location such as GPS (the app has no location permission).
  • Data is kept for 1 year and is not provided to third parties.

7. Permissions

The app does not request permissions for location, camera, microphone, contacts, reading or writing photos and files, or accounts. Internet access (ads and statistics), checking network state, access to the advertising ID, and keeping the screen on are needed by the app and its included SDKs. They do not read your personal information.

8. App updates

When the app tells you about a new version, it uses Google Play's update feature. The developer does not receive your information through this.

9. Children under 14

The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

10. Your rights

The app has no accounts, so the developer holds no personally identifying data about you. You can delete on-device data at any time:

  • In the app: "Clear Saved Phrases" or deleting individual presets on the Presets screen
  • Device settings: App info → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app
  • Automatic backup file (led_board_backup.json): delete it from your Downloads folder with a file manager (it may remain after you uninstall the app)
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in device Settings → Google → Ads
