Privacy Policy — Camera Ruler

한국어

Effective date: October 27, 2026

App name: Camera Ruler ("카메라 줄자")

This policy explains what the app processes and where it sends it.

1. Principles (no sign-up; camera video stays on your device; no operator server stores your measurements)

The app has no accounts or sign-in, and the operator runs no server or database that receives your measured values or record names. The app requests one permission only: camera. It does not request location, contacts, photos, SMS, or microphone permissions.

2. What is processed

a. Camera video. The app shows the camera view to measure length, and the video is processed only on your device. The app does not store camera video or photos and does not send them to the operator's server or to any third party. Saved records contain no video.

b. Google Play Services for AR (ARCore). The app uses Google's Google Play Services for AR (ARCore) to find surfaces and place points in the camera view. This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Use of ARCore functionality in the app is subject to the then-current Google Terms of Service (https://policies.google.com/terms) and Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). How ARCore handles data: https://support.google.com/ar?p=how-google-play-services-for-ar-handles-your-data. Google may process device identifiers and diagnostics for ARCore usage analytics; the app operator does not receive this. The app does not use ARCore Cloud Anchors or the Geospatial API. On devices that do not support ARCore the app still installs; the measure screen shows a notice and only History and Guide work.

c. Stored on your device (never sent to the operator's server) — record names you chose, measured values (millimetre totals and segments), the "unstable" flag, save time; settings (theme, language, length unit). Stored in on-device storage and removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Backup file: when records or settings change, the app automatically writes a backup file (JSON containing the records and settings above) to your device's shared Downloads folder, and "Back up to file" in Settings writes additional files. These files stay on the device and are not sent anywhere by the app, but because they sit in a shared folder they may remain after uninstall unless you delete them, and other apps or file managers with file access on your device can see them.

d. Sharing (only when you tap). Tapping "Share" on a record passes a text of its name, value and segments to the Android share sheet, and only to the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you tap. A rewarded ad may play before the share sheet opens (if the ad finishes, the sheet opens; if no ad loads, it opens right away); ad processing is described in e below.

e. Monitoring and advertising data sent automatically

  • Owl Pulse (our own usage monitoring) → https://api.owlmakers.com/v1/pulse. Sent: app package name, version, language; a random session ID created at each launch; a random install ID created once at installation (not an account or hardware ID — a new value after uninstall); code-style IDs of screens and button taps (e.g. measure.add_point); action outcomes (success / failure / cancel with a category code); ad shown / click / failure signals; the record number (a sequence number, not the name or value) when a record is deleted; an "app is open" signal. Not sent: measured values, record names, camera video, or point coordinates. Debug builds do not send; only released builds do. The server derives only the country/region/city name from the connection for statistics and does not store the IP address or coordinates.
  • Google AdMob (ads): advertising ID, IP address, device information, and ad interactions may be processed to show banner ads, native ads (list, record detail, guide), the exit-screen ad, an interstitial ad after you save a measurement, and the rewarded ad before sharing. No ads appear while you are measuring (camera screen). Where consent is required (EEA, UK, etc.) the Google UMP consent form appears first.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (GA4): anonymous usage statistics (screen views, button-tap codes, outcomes), device model and OS version. The Firebase SDK may create a per-installation random identifier (Firebase installation ID). Measured values and record names are not sent.

3. Retention

Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data; backup files in Downloads must be deleted by you. Monitoring, analytics, advertising and ARCore data are kept under each system's retention policy and then used only as aggregated statistics.

4. Sharing

The app does not provide your measured values, record names, or camera video to anyone. Third parties involved: Google LLC (Google Play Services for AR / ARCore, AdMob, Firebase — https://policies.google.com/privacy) and OwlMakers (own Pulse monitoring).

5. Your choices

Delete records one by one (undo is available for 5 seconds), use "Delete all records" in Settings, clear app data, or uninstall to remove on-device data; delete backup files in Downloads with a file manager. Revoke camera permission in system settings to stop measuring (History and Guide keep working). Where consent is required, change your ad consent under Settings → Ad privacy settings; elsewhere use Google ad settings on your device.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above is updated; material changes are announced in the app or the store listing.
