Privacy Policy — Card Spend Goal & Bill Tracker

Effective: October 20, 2026 · 한국어

Owlmakers explains how "Card Spend Goal & Bill Tracker" (Korean title: 카드 실적 관리 - 정기결제·고정지출, com.owlmakers.cardusage) handles information.

1. Local financial data

Cards, monthly targets, recurring expenses and spending entries are stored on your device. The app has no accounts and no backend database, and this data is never sent to our servers (the only exception is the optional Family link in section 2-1, which you must turn on yourself). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. Backup files you create are plain JSON saved where you choose.

2. SMS and notifications

When you run "Import messages", "Find cards from messages", or open the app, card approval/cancellation/payment text messages (date, amount, merchant, card, and the cardholder name that the card company masks in the text, e.g. “홍*동”) are read and matched in memory only. If you turn on "Register instantly when a message arrives", only messages that look like card payments are kept in the app's private storage (excluded from auto-backup) until they are processed, then deleted; other messages are discarded immediately.

If you turn on "Read payments from notifications", Android gives the app access to all notifications, but the app reads only payment notifications from KakaoBank, Toss Bank and card-company notices in KakaoTalk, and discards every other notification without reading its content. Payment notifications are held in the same private storage only until processed, then deleted (anything unprocessed is deleted after at most 3 days).

Raw message and notification text is never sent to any server. Only the final amount, date, merchant and card information you register (including the masked cardholder name) is stored on your device. The masked cardholder name is kept exactly as received, never unmasked or guessed by the app, and is used only to tell similar cards (such as family cards) apart; it is also included in the backup files you create.

Card photo (optional): the photo you choose is cropped to a card shape and only the cropped result is saved in the app's private storage. The original photo is not kept, location and other photo details do not carry over, and nothing is sent to any server.

2-1. Family link (optional)

Only if you turn on Settings → Family link and pair with another person's phone, the payments of the cards you choose to share (date, time, amount, merchant, approval number, category, and for foreign-currency payments the original currency, foreign amount and whether the conversion is an estimate), a description of those cards (issuer, nickname, last four digits), the display name you pick, and approval flags are relayed to the other phone. Raw SMS/notification text is never relayed. Pairing needs a 12-character invite code created on one phone and typed on the other; the sender and the receiver must each approve, and new cards are not shared by default.

The relayed data is end-to-end encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a secret key created on your phone, wrapped with the invite code and held only on the two phones in Android's secure storage (not included in automatic backups). Neither we nor Google can read the contents. The mailbox is Google Cloud Firestore (Seoul region), run by Google — we run no server. Google may process content-independent data such as a random anonymous sign-in ID, a random per-install number, and upload time/size.

Retention: the receiving phone deletes a message right after collecting it; the sending phone's app deletes anything uncollected within 30 days at the latest (longer if the app is not opened). An invite code stops working after 10 minutes and its document is deleted when accepted; an unaccepted invite document may remain, encrypted. Disconnecting deletes your key, your messages and your control document (if that fails, e.g. offline, the app retries later); the encrypted "left" marker of the side that disconnects first is deleted when the other side disconnects (if the other side just uninstalls, one encrypted control document may remain). A payment already received stays on the receiving phone like any other entry (even after disconnecting) until you delete it there, so pair only with people you trust. You can turn sending/receiving off, pause, or disconnect in Settings → Family link.

3. Services that communicate

  • Google AdMob (top banner, in-list native, exit-screen native): advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions, handled under Google's policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). Where consent is required (EEA/UK), Google UMP asks first and ads do not start before it; you can change the choice in Settings.
  • Firebase anonymous sign-in and Cloud Firestore (only if Family link is on): carry the end-to-end encrypted mailbox data described in section 2-1; Google handles them under its policies (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: screen views and basic interaction events, device model and OS version (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Owl Pulse (api.owlmakers.com): app package name, version, display language, a random per-launch session ID, screen/button event codes, ad-click flags and a 15-second heartbeat. Our server derives only the country/region/city name from the connection; the IP address and coordinates are not stored. No financial data, card numbers, amounts, SMS text, names or contacts are sent.

4. Retention and your choices

On-device data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. You can turn off notification reading in Settings, and revoke it in Android Settings → Notifications → Device & app notifications (notification access); SMS permission can be revoked in Android app permissions, and the app keeps working with manual entry. Analytics and ad data are kept under each provider's retention policy and used in aggregate.
