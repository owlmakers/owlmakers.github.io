Privacy Policy — Card Spend Goal & Bill Tracker

Effective: October 23, 2026 · 한국어

Owlmakers explains how "Card Spend Goal & Bill Tracker" (Korean title: 카드 실적 관리 - 정기결제·고정지출, com.owlmakers.cardusage) handles information.

1. Local financial data

Cards, monthly targets, recurring expenses and spending entries are stored on your device. The app has no accounts and no backend database, and this data is never sent to our servers (the only exception is the optional Phone link in section 2-1, which you must turn on yourself). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. Backup files you create are plain JSON saved where you choose.

2. SMS and notifications

When you run "Import messages", "Find cards from messages", or open the app, card approval/cancellation/payment text messages (date, amount, merchant, card, and the cardholder name that the card company masks in the text, e.g. “홍*동”) are read and matched in memory only. If you turn on "Register instantly when a message arrives", only messages that look like card payments are kept in the app's private storage (excluded from auto-backup) until they are processed, then deleted; other messages are discarded immediately.

If you turn on "Read payments from notifications", Android gives the app access to all notifications, but the app checks each notification on your device and reads only those that look like a card payment (a card-company name, an approval or payment word and an amount together; this includes KakaoBank and Toss Bank payment notifications and card-company notices in KakaoTalk), and discards every other notification immediately without storing or logging it. Notifications from payment-intermediary apps, messaging apps, SMS apps and the system are not read. Payment notifications are held in the same private storage only until processed, then deleted (anything unprocessed is deleted after at most 3 days).

Raw message and notification text is never sent to any server. Only the final amount, date, merchant and card information you register (including the masked cardholder name) is stored on your device. The masked cardholder name is kept exactly as received, never unmasked or guessed by the app, and is used only to tell similar cards (such as family cards) apart; it is also included in the backup files you create.

Card photo (optional): the photo you choose is cropped to a card shape and only the cropped result is saved in the app's private storage. The original photo is not kept, location and other photo details do not carry over, and nothing is sent to any server.

2-1. Phone link (optional)

Only if you turn on Settings → Phone link and pair with another person's phone, the payments of the cards you choose to share (date, time, amount, merchant, approval number, category, and for foreign-currency payments the original currency, foreign amount and whether the conversion is an estimate), a description of those cards (issuer, nickname, last four digits), the display name you pick, and approval flags are relayed to the other phone. Raw SMS/notification text is never relayed. Pairing needs a 12-character invite code created on one phone and typed on the other; the sender and the receiver must each approve, and new cards are not shared by default.

The relayed data is end-to-end encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a secret key created on your phone, wrapped with the invite code and held only on the two phones in Android's secure storage (not included in automatic backups). Neither we nor Google can read the contents. The mailbox is Google Cloud Firestore (Seoul region), run by Google — we run no server of our own; only a small function that sends the wake-up signal described below runs on Google's cloud. Google may process content-independent data such as a random anonymous sign-in ID, a random per-install number, and upload time/size.

Wake-up signal (push): when the other phone uploads a payment or changes the link state, a small Google-run cloud function (Cloud Functions) sends your phone only a "there is something new, please check" signal through Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). The signal carries no content such as payments or names. For this, your phone's push address (an FCM registration token, a random per-install string) is stored unencrypted in the link's control document and read by the function. It is deleted together with the control document when you disconnect, and the function removes tokens that are no longer valid. If a signal cannot be received (app force-stopped, battery restrictions), the app fetches hourly or when you open it.

Retention: the receiving phone deletes a message right after collecting it; the sending phone's app deletes anything uncollected within 30 days at the latest (longer if the app is not opened). An invite code stops working after 10 minutes and its document is deleted when accepted; an unaccepted invite document may remain, encrypted. Disconnecting deletes your key, your messages and your control document (if that fails, e.g. offline, the app retries later); the encrypted "left" marker of the side that disconnects first is deleted when the other side disconnects (if the other side just uninstalls, one encrypted control document may remain). A payment already received stays on the receiving phone like any other entry (even after disconnecting) until you delete it there, so pair only with people you trust. You can turn sending/receiving off, pause, or disconnect in Settings → Phone link.

2-2. Sending notification shapes (optional, off by default)

Only if you turn on Settings → "Send notification shapes (anonymous)", the app sends the developer the anonymous shape of notifications so it can learn more card companies' payment formats. In a shape, numbers are replaced with # and everything except format words (card-company names, approval/installment words, "원", USD and similar) is replaced with ▢ plus the letter count (example: ▢4 ##,###원 일시불). What is sent: the notifying app's package name, the masked shape, a label of how it was found (same amount as a payment text message / chosen by you / already read / looks like a payment), the app version and the time. Never sent: raw notification or message text, amounts, merchants, names, card or phone numbers; masked characters cannot be recovered.

While this is on, amount digits are kept only in memory for up to 20 minutes to check whether a payment text message with the same amount arrived nearby (never written to disk or sent). Notifications from messaging apps, SMS apps, payment-intermediary apps, the system and this app are not collected. Masked shapes wait in the app's private storage (excluded from auto-backup; at most 80 items for 30 days) and are deleted after being sent. You can review them before they are sent in Settings → "Notification shapes to send", or choose a payment notification yourself in "I paid but it was not recorded".

Shapes are written to a write-only collection in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Seoul) using anonymous sign-in (no name or email); the app cannot read it back, only the developer can view it, and it is not linked to personal information. Turning the switch off stops collection and deletes shapes still stored on your device; shapes already sent are anonymous and cannot be traced to a device, so individual deletion requests are hard to fulfil.

3. Services that communicate

  • Google AdMob (top banner, in-list native, exit-screen native): advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions, handled under Google's policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). Where consent is required (EEA/UK), Google UMP asks first and ads do not start before it; you can change the choice in Settings.
  • Firebase anonymous sign-in and Cloud Firestore (only if Phone link is on): carry the end-to-end encrypted mailbox data described in section 2-1, and Cloud Functions and Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver the content-free wake-up signal (this phone's push token is stored unencrypted for that purpose); Google handles them under its policies (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: screen views and basic interaction events, device model and OS version (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Owl Pulse (api.owlmakers.com): app package name, version, display language, a random per-launch session ID, screen/button event codes, ad-click flags and a 15-second heartbeat. Our server derives only the country/region/city name from the connection; the IP address and coordinates are not stored. No financial data, card numbers, amounts, SMS text, names or contacts are sent.

4. Retention and your choices

On-device data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. You can turn off notification reading in Settings, and revoke it in Android Settings → Notifications → Device & app notifications (notification access); SMS permission can be revoked in Android app permissions, and the app keeps working with manual entry. Analytics and ad data are kept under each provider's retention policy and used in aggregate.
