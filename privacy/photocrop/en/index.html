Privacy Policy — Photo Crop - Batch & Ratio

Last updated: October 13, 2026 · 한국어

Owlmakers ("the Developer") operates "Photo Crop - Batch & Ratio" ("the App"). This policy explains what the App does with your information.

Key points
· Your photos are processed only on your device. Photos, file names and photo locations are never sent to the Developer's servers or to third parties.
· The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and the Developer runs no server for it.
· The App does not request full storage access, camera, location, microphone or contacts permissions. It only reads the photos you choose in the Android photo picker.
· The App uses ads (Google AdMob) and anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase). Photo content and file names are not part of them.

1. Photos — they never leave your device

  • The App reads only the photos you pick in the photo picker. Cropping and saving happen entirely on your device.
  • Original photos are never overwritten. Cropped results are saved as new files in your photo library (Pictures/PhotoCrop folder).
  • New photos saved by the App do not include the original's EXIF metadata such as GPS location. Orientation is applied to the saved image.
  • Photos, file names, photo addresses (URIs) and locations are never put into ad requests or usage statistics.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is not sent to the Developer.

ItemContent
SettingsLast used ratio, output format (JPEG/PNG) and quality, theme (system/light/dark), display language, whether the first-run guide was seen
Work in progressAddresses (URIs), file names and sizes of the photos you picked, the crop area coordinates you set per photo, and processing status (waiting, done, failed, cancelled). Stored so you can resume after the app is interrupted.
Results listFile names and saved photo addresses of recent results (up to 100)
Backup filephoto_crop_backup.json, containing the settings, work and results list above. It is saved to your device's Downloads folder whenever the data changes; tapping "Back up to file now" in Settings creates a dated copy. The file does not contain the photos themselves, stays on your device and is not sent to the Developer. You can delete it with a file manager.

3. Ads — Google AdMob

The App is free and shows Google AdMob ads.

  • Ads may appear in a banner at the top of the screen and in the exit confirmation window. The App does not show full-screen ads in the middle of cropping or saving.
  • AdMob may collect advertising ID, IP address, app interactions and diagnostics to serve ads. This follows Google's policies; the Developer does not separately collect or store it. Photos and file names are not passed to ads.
  • How Google handles ad data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Ad personalization: change or reset your advertising ID under device Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Users in the European Economic Area and the UK see an ad consent (UMP) dialog at first launch, and the ad SDK does not start until consent is handled. You can change your choice under "Ad privacy settings" in Settings (shown only in those regions).

4. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics for improvement and stability.

  • Collected — which screen was opened (Home, Crop, Results, Settings); how many photos were picked (a count); which ratio was chosen (e.g. square); how many results succeeded, failed or were cancelled and the output format; whether a saved result was opened or shared. Also a randomly assigned app instance ID and what Google Analytics collects automatically (sessions, approximate region from IP address, device and app version, advertising ID, etc.).
  • Not collected — photos, file names, photo addresses, photo locations, or personally identifying information such as name, email or phone number.
  • Processor — Google collects and processes the data. The Developer only views aggregated statistics in which individuals cannot be identified. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Developer servers

The App sends no information to any server operated by the Developer. Because there are no accounts, the Developer holds no personal information.

6. Permissions

The App does not request full storage read/write, camera, location, microphone, contacts or account permissions. Photos are read only from the system photo picker (Android 13+) or file picker (Android 12 and below) after you choose them.

Internet access, network state and advertising ID access are needed by the ad and analytics SDKs; they do not let the App read your photos or files.

7. App updates

New-version prompts use Google Play's in-app update feature. The Developer receives no user information in this process.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Your choices and deleting data

The Developer holds no personally identifying information. You can delete on-device data at any time (the App has no separate delete button):

  • Device settings: App info → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the App
  • Backup files (photo_crop_backup*.json): delete from the Downloads folder with a file manager (they may remain after uninstalling)
  • Saved result photos: delete from the PhotoCrop album in your gallery
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete under device Settings → Google → Ads
