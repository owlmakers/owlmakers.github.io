DashMeter Privacy Policy

Effective 17 August 2026 · Publisher: Owlmakers · 한국어

Owlmakers ("the developer") operates DashMeter ("the app"), which uses GPS to show your speed, trip distance and acceleration / braking performance. It is an on-device app with no server, no sign-in and no cloud.

Summary
· Location (GPS) is used only to compute speed and coordinates are never sent to a server.
· Location is used only while the app is open (no background tracking).
· There is no account or sign-in, so the developer holds no personal data about you.
· Ads (Google AdMob) use an advertising ID; purchasing the unlock removes ads entirely.

1. Location — it never leaves your device

The app uses your device's GPS location to compute speed, trip distance and acceleration / braking measurements.

  • Location coordinates are never sent to the developer's servers or to any third party.
  • Location is used on-device for speed calculation only; raw coordinates are not stored.
  • Location is used only while the app is open. The app does not request background location permission and stops receiving location when you leave the app.
  • The app still runs if you decline location permission — only speed measurement is unavailable.

2. Data stored on your device

The following stays on your device only and is never transmitted.

ItemContents
Measurement records0-100 / 100-0 results with the GPS accuracy and sample rate of each run
App settingsUnits (km/h / mph), theme, language, unlock (purchase) state

Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app shows Google AdMob ads to remain free (on the Measure and Settings screens only; the drive screen and HUD carry no ads).

  • AdMob may collect an advertising ID (AAID) and device information to serve ads. This is governed by Google's policies; the developer does not separately collect or retain it.
  • The developer never provides location coordinates for advertising purposes.
  • Google advertising data: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Ad personalisation can be changed in device Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Users in the EEA and the UK are shown a consent (UMP) dialog on first launch.
  • Purchasing the unlock removes ads entirely.

4. Payments

The measurement unlock is processed through Google Play in-app billing. Payment details are handled by Google; the developer never receives or sees them. Only the fact that a purchase exists is stored on your device.

5. Children under 14

The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

6. Your rights

The app has no accounts and no sign-in, so the developer holds no personal data about you. You can clear on-device data at any time by deleting records in the app or uninstalling it.

7. Safety notice

This app is for reference and does not replace your vehicle's instrument cluster. Do not look at the screen while driving; use interactive features only when stopped. This app is not a speed-trap avoidance tool.
