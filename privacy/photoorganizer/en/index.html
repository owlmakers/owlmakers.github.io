Photo Organizer Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026 · Publisher: Owlmakers · 한국어

Owlmakers ("the developer") operates Photo Organizer ("the app"). The app helps you group and clean up screenshots, burst shots, similar photos, and downloaded images. It is an on-device app with no accounts and no cloud upload.

Summary
· Your photos, thumbnails, filenames, and metadata never leave your device — all grouping and sorting run locally on your phone.
· There are no accounts or sign-ups; the developer holds no personal information about you.
· Moving photos to the trash or deleting them only happens when you confirm via Android's official system dialog.
· Ads (Google AdMob) use your Advertising ID, and anonymous analytics/crash reports (Firebase) never include your photos.

1. Photos and Media — they never leave your device

The app uses your device's photo and media read permissions (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older Android versions, and optional ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION) to group screenshots, burst shots, similar photos, and downloaded images.

  • Your photos, thumbnails, filenames, timestamps, and metadata are never sent to the developer's servers or to any third party.
  • All photo grouping, similarity detection, and sorting run 100% on your device (on-device).
  • Moving photos to the trash or deleting them is performed only when you explicitly confirm via Android's official system confirmation dialog (MediaStore). On Android 12+, if you yourself allow this app under system Settings > "Media management apps" (MANAGE_MEDIA permission), that confirmation dialog is skipped and the action you tapped runs right away, with an undo option.
  • Media management permission (MANAGE_MEDIA, Android 12+, optional) — used only to reduce per-action system confirmation dialogs when moving photos into Pictures/PhotoOrganizer/ folders or to the trash. It takes effect only if you allow it in Settings; without it the app still works and asks for confirmation. It is not used to read photos or metadata and send them anywhere; it only changes file locations on your device.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app displays Google AdMob ads to remain free.

  • AdMob may collect an advertising ID (AAID) and device information to serve ads.
  • This is governed by Google's policies; the developer does not separately collect or retain it.
  • The developer never shares your photos or media data for advertising purposes.
  • Google advertising data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Ad personalisation can be changed in device Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Users in the EEA and the UK are shown a consent (UMP) dialog on first launch.

4. Usage analytics & crash reporting (Firebase Analytics)

This app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics so the app can be improved.

  • What is collected — anonymous app usage events and a randomly assigned app instance ID per installation.
  • What is not collected — no personally identifying information (name, email, phone number) and none of your photos or media files.
  • Who processes it — Google performs the collection and processing; the developer only views aggregated statistics in which individuals are not identifiable. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Children under 14

The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

6. Your rights

The app has no accounts and no sign-in, so the developer holds no personal data about you. You can clear on-device data at any time by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.
