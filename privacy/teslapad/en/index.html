Privacy Policy — TeslPad

Last updated: November 19, 2026 (app version 1.0.6) · 한국어

Owlmakers explains how "TeslPad" (com.owlmakers.teslapad) handles information. This is an unofficial third-party app. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or related to Tesla, Inc. Tesla and related names are trademarks of their owner.

Summary
· The app talks to your vehicle directly over Bluetooth. Your vehicle identification number (VIN) and the vehicle authentication key stay on your phone and are never sent to a server.
· The location permission is requested only because Android needs it for Bluetooth scanning. The app does not read, store or send a location value (coordinates).
· No accounts or sign-in.
· Ads (using the advertising ID), anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase and our own usage monitoring), abnormal-event diagnostics and push-notification subscriptions communicate with outside services. Sections 3 to 7 describe each one.

1. Information stored only on your device (not sent to the developer's servers)

ItemDetails

2. Backup file

Whenever your settings change, the app saves a bundle of settings as a JSON file in the teslapad folder under the phone's Download folder. You can also create one yourself with "Back up to file now" in Settings. It exists so you can restore with "Restore from file" after reinstalling.

  • Contents: the settings, panel, automations and power-graph history from section 1, the name and address of the car Bluetooth device, and the random install ID used for usage statistics (section 5).
  • Not included: the VIN and the vehicle authentication key.
  • The file stays on your phone and is not sent to the developer's servers. It remains after you uninstall the app, and you can delete it with a file manager. Android's system auto-backup (allowBackup) is not used.

4. Ads

The app is free and shows ads.

  • Ads are loaded through Unity LevelPlay ad mediation. The app includes connectors for ad networks that LevelPlay can use (Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, Pangle, Mintegral, Liftoff Monetize, InMobi, DT Exchange) and the Google ads SDK. Which networks actually serve ads depends on the LevelPlay configuration.
  • To serve ads, the ad SDKs and networks may collect or share the advertising ID (a device identifier), the IP address and the approximate region estimated from it, device and app information, ad impressions and interactions, and ad-loading diagnostics. The VIN, vehicle information and location values are not passed to ads.
  • Ads appear in a banner at the top of the screen and between sections of several screens. Full-screen ads appear only when the car is parked or not connected.
  • In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area and the UK, consent is requested first through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and ads are loaded afterwards. In those regions you can change your choice with "Ad privacy settings" in Settings.
  • You can delete or reset the advertising ID, or turn off personalized ads, in your device's Settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • How Google handles ad data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Usage statistics

5-1. Google Analytics for Firebase

We collect screen changes and feature usage (identification codes of buttons and actions, and success or failure result codes) as statistics. The VIN, the vehicle authentication key, location and vehicle status values are not put into events. Google may process information the SDK collects automatically, such as the app instance ID, device and app information and the approximate region from the IP address, and we do not guarantee that it is fully anonymous. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5-2. Our own usage monitoring (Owlmakers)

To check that the app is being used normally, the app sends anonymous usage statistics to our own server (https://api.owlmakers.com/v1/pulse, HTTPS).

  • What is sent: app name, version and display language; screen changes and button identification codes; a signal that the app is in use; ad-loading times and result codes; a random session value created at each launch; and a random install ID that is created once and stored on the phone (used only to follow the same installation across days. It is not linked to a name, account or hardware identifier. A new one is created if you clear the app's data or uninstall. Restoring a backup file may bring the same value back).
  • From the connection, the server records only the country, region and city names. It does not store the IP address or latitude and longitude.
  • The VIN, vehicle keys, vehicle status values, names and contact details are not sent.
  • Data is kept for one year and then deleted automatically by the server. It is not provided to third parties.

6. Abnormal-event diagnostics

Only when something goes wrong in the communication with your car (stalls, timeouts, unexpected responses, internal errors), the app sends a short diagnostic line describing the situation (error type, numeric codes, internal error location) to the server in 5-2. Normal activity is not sent, and the volume is limited to 30 lines per run and 6 lines per minute. Lines containing a VIN, vehicle name, key or coordinates are not sent. Retention is the same as in 5-2.

7. Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

We use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to receive announcements from the developer. When you open the app, Google assigns a registration token to this installation, and the app subscribes to two notification topics: one for the app and one for your display language. The app does not send or store this token on the developer's servers. Notifications go to all users of the same app and language with the same content and contain no personal information. On Android 13 and later, the notification permission is requested only when you tap the in-app prompt or the notification row in Settings, or when you turn on "Notify me when my car is nearby", and every feature except notifications works without it.

8. App update check

The app uses Google Play's in-app update feature to check whether a new version is available. Google Play handles this check.

9. Deletion and retention

  • Settings, vehicle information and the key on your device: clear them with App info → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app. Delete the key registered on the car separately on the car's screen.
  • Backup and diagnostic files: they remain after uninstalling, so delete them with a file manager.
  • Because there are no accounts and the developer cannot identify users, we do not offer a separate procedure to look up and delete a specific user's information. Server usage statistics are deleted automatically after one year. Information processed by Google SDKs follows Google's policies and your ad and analytics settings.
