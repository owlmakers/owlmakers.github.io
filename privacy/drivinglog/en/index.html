Privacy Policy — Vehicle Mileage Log

Effective: November 19, 2026 · Published by Owlmakers

Owlmakers ("we") operates the "Mileage Log" app (package com.owlmakers.drivinglog, "the app"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Information we do not collect

  • The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account. There is no sign-up or login.
  • The app does not collect location. It does not request a location permission, and trip records have no latitude/longitude field. Distance is calculated from the two odometer readings you type.
  • The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

2. Information stored only on your device

  • Vehicles (name, plate, current reading), trips (date, start and end reading, purpose, from/to place names, note) and settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to our servers.
  • Backup files (JSON) are created in your device storage, such as the Downloads folder, and are not sent to our servers. Uninstalling the app may leave backup files in Downloads; delete them yourself if you no longer need them.

3. Exporting files

  • When you export a monthly log (PDF or CSV) or a backup file, your device's share sheet opens and the file goes only to the app or location you choose. We do not receive or read exported files.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob and Unity LevelPlay)

  • The app shows ads served through Google AdMob, the ad mediation service Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks connected through them.
  • To serve ads and prevent invalid traffic, ad providers may collect and process your advertising ID, device information, approximate location (IP-based) and app usage data. We do not receive or store this information.
  • Where consent is required (EEA, UK and similar regions), a consent form (Google UMP) appears before ads start and can be reopened from "Ad privacy settings" in Settings.
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • To reset ad personalization: device Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

  • To improve the app we use Google Analytics for Firebase to count which screens are opened and which features are used. Events are recorded with an automatically assigned app instance ID and are not linked to your identity.
  • Kilometre readings, place names and plate numbers you enter are not sent. Only screen names and feature names leave the device.
  • How Google processes analytics data: https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245

6. Our own usage statistics (Owlmakers)

  • To check that the app is working, it sends anonymous usage statistics to our server (api.owlmakers.com, HTTPS).
  • Sent: app name, version and display language, screen and feature names, a signal that the app is in use, and a random value created once per installation and kept on the device. It is not an account or hardware identifier; uninstalling the app or clearing its data creates a new one.
  • The server records only country, region and city names derived from the connection; it does not store IP addresses or coordinates.
  • Kilometre readings, place names and plate numbers are not sent here either. We do not share these statistics with third parties.

7. News notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

  • Right after you first open the app, it shows an explanation asking whether you want news as notifications, and asks for the Android notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) only if you agree. You can also turn "News notifications" on or off in Settings. If you decline or dismiss it, the app asks again on later launches at growing intervals (1, 3, then 7 days, at most 5 times) and stops once you allow it. Firebase Cloud Messaging processes a device token to deliver them, and the app subscribes only to two topics: the app and your display language. They have nothing to do with your trip records.

8. Children

  • The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Deleting data

  • Data on your device is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in system settings (delete backup and exported files in Downloads yourself). For questions about statistics, contact us below.

10. Changes

  • This policy may be updated as laws or the service change; changes will be posted on this page.

11. Contact

  • Privacy questions: support@owlmakers.com
