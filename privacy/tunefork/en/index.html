Tunefork Privacy Policy

Effective: 17 August 2026 · Publisher: Owlmakers · 한국어

Owlmakers ("the developer") operates Tunefork ("the app"), which measures the pitch of your instrument to help you tune it and provides a metronome. It is an on-device app with no server, no account and no cloud.

Summary
· The microphone is used only to calculate pitch; nothing is recorded or transmitted.
· There is no developer-operated server, so no user information is collected or stored.
· Advertising (Google AdMob) may use device identifiers, and a one-time purchase removes ads entirely.

1. Information we do not collect

The app does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number or postal address. There is no sign-up, and no user information is sent to any server operated by the developer.

2. Microphone — nothing is recorded

The app uses the microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) to work out the pitch of your instrument.

  • Microphone input is used only for pitch calculation and is discarded immediately afterwards.
  • No recording is created, nothing is stored on your device, and nothing is transmitted anywhere.
  • All analysis happens on your device.
  • The app stops using the microphone as soon as you leave the tuning screen or send the app to the background.

3. Settings stored on your device

Your chosen instrument, reference pitch, metronome settings, language and theme are stored locally for convenience. These values never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows Google AdMob advertising so it can be used for free. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the Advertising ID in order to serve ads. This processing is carried out by Google; the developer neither collects nor stores that information.

  • Google advertising policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.
  • Ad content is restricted to a family-safe (G) rating.
  • A one-time purchase removes advertising entirely.

5. In-app purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. The developer never receives or stores payment details such as card numbers, and only checks with Google Play whether a purchase exists in order to unlock features.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed primarily at children under 14. Because instrument practice apps are often used with children present, ad content is restricted as described above.

7. Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page.
