Stampshot Privacy Policy

Effective 17 August 2026 · Published by Owlmakers · 한국어

Owlmakers ("the developer") operates Stampshot ("the app"), a camera that writes the date and time onto your photos.

The developer collects no personal data. There is no server, no account, no sign-up and no login. Your photos, your location and your settings stay on your device.

1. Handled on your device only

DataPurposeStored where
Photos you takeStamping the date and time onto the photoDevice storage (gallery)
Location (optional)Only to write coordinates/address onto the photoInside the photo file
App settingsStamp fields, position, size, colour, save folder, language, themeDevice storage

About location — if you turn the location stamp on, the app reads your device's last known position and writes it onto the photo. Those coordinates are never transmitted to the developer. If you also enable the address line, the coordinates are passed to the address lookup service provided by the Android operating system (Google). Declining the location permission leaves every other feature — shooting and saving included — fully working.

2. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

To show ads, Google may collect your Advertising ID along with basic device and app information. Google is the party collecting and using that data; the developer does not receive it. Ad content rating is limited to General audiences (G). See the Google advertising policy. You can reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Permissions

PermissionWhen it is requestedIf you decline
CameraOnly when you choose silent modeBest-quality mode keeps working without it
LocationOnly when you enable the location or address stampOnly those stamp lines are omitted
InternetShowing adsAds do not appear; shooting and saving work as usual

The app never asks for permissions on first launch. It asks at the moment a feature actually needs one.

4. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

5. Retention and deletion

Because the developer holds no user data, there is no deletion request procedure. Settings stored on your device are removed when you uninstall the app, and photos can be deleted from your gallery, or several at once from the app's library screen.

6. Changes and contact

Any change will be posted on this page with its date. Contact: support@owlmakers.com
