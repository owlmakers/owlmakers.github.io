AppRotate Privacy Policy — Owlmakers

Effective: 10 September 2026 · Publisher: Owlmakers · 한국어

Last updated: 2026-09-10

1. Summary

AppRotate has no account and no sign-in. The app does not run a server, and everything you configure in it — the apps you pick, their order, the interval, the automation options — is stored only on your device. The developer cannot see any of it.

The only things the app sends out are ads and anonymous usage statistics, both described below.

2. Stored on your device only (never transmitted)

ItemWhy
Package names and order of the apps you pickedSo the next launch continues with the same setup
Interval and automation settings (Bluetooth device, screen-off, reset on interruption)To keep the behaviour you chose
Theme and language choice, whether you have seen the introductionFor display

These values live in Android's app-private storage and are deleted when you uninstall the app. They are not sent to the developer, and the developer has no way to read them.

3. List of installed apps

So that you can choose which apps to rotate, the app queries the list of apps that appear in your launcher. That list is used only to show you the choices and is never transmitted. The package names you select are stored on the device as described in section 2.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads. To serve them, Google may collect your advertising ID (a device identifier) and coarse device information.

  • Google's practices: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Users in the European Economic Area and the UK are shown a consent request on first launch,

and the choice can be changed again from the app's settings.

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in

Device Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To see which screens are used and which settings are common, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase in anonymous, aggregated form.

  • What is sent: screen names, the kind of action (rotation started/stopped and similar),

the interval value, and the number of apps selected.

  • ⛔ What is not sent: the names or package names of the apps you picked, the list of apps

installed on your device, or any personally identifying information such as contacts, location, phone number or e-mail.

6. Permissions

PermissionPurpose
Display over other appsTo bring an app to the front in the order you set (nothing is drawn on top of other apps)
Ignore battery optimisationsSo switching does not stop while the screen is off
NotificationsTo show the current state and to control it (pause, resume, adjust interval)
Usage access (optional)To reset the timer when you open another app yourself. Evaluated on the device only; never transmitted
Bluetooth connection state (optional)To start or stop automatically when a chosen device connects

7. Children

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Your choices

  • Uninstalling the app deletes every setting stored on the device.
  • Personalised ads can be turned off and the advertising ID reset in your device settings.
  • The optional permissions (usage access, Bluetooth) can be declined without affecting the

app's core behaviour.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the "last updated" date above will be updated.
